HeatWatch-813: Autonomous Multi-Sensor GeoAI Engine

Urban Expansion & Extreme Heat Vulnerability Intelligence across Arab Cities

1. The Crisis

Rapid metropolitan growth across Arab coastal plains creates extreme Urban Heat Islands where surface temperatures exceed 50°C, threatening public health and straining electric power grids.

2. The Optical Barrier

Standard multispectral imagery (RGB + NIR) cannot distinguish high-albedo cool roofs from weathered concrete or aged asphalt due to wide channel averaging and quartz sand spectral confusion.

3. The 813 Breakthrough

Arab Hyperspectral Satellite 813 (205 narrow contiguous bands, 400–1700 nm) resolves subtle hydrocarbon, mineral, and elastomeric spectral fingerprints at 20 m spatial resolution.
